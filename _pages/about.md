- Ph.D Candidate in Computer Science at [Université Laval](https://www.ulaval.ca/), Canada, under the supervision of Professor [Audrey Durand](https://mila.quebec/fr/annuaire/audrey-durand). I am also a PhD Researcher at the [Mila – Quebec AI Institute](https://mila.quebec/fr) and Student Researcher affiliated with the [Institut Intelligence et Données (IID)](https://iid.ulaval.ca/). Previously, I obtained a Master's degree in Data Science from the [African Institute for Mathematical Sciences, Rwanda](https://aims.ac.rw/). Before that, I jointly earned two Bachelor's degrees from the [Université d'Abomey-Calavi](https://www.uac.bj/), Benin: a B.Sc. in Computer Science (Software Engineering) from the [Institut de Formation et de Recherche en Informatique (IFRI)](https://ifri-uac.bj/) and a B.Sc. in Applied Mathematics from the Faculty of Science and Technology (FAST). For more details about my background and experience, see my [CV](/files/Christophe_Hounanou_CV.pdf).

- My research focuses on reinforcement learning, with an emphasis on multi-agent systems and emergent communication. I am particularly interested in how cooperative agents can learn to communicate and coordinate effectively in decentralized environments. My work explores the intersection of multi-agent reinforcement learning, probabilistic pragmatic reasoning, and language-based agents, with the goal of developing principled methods for communication and decision-making in collaborative AI systems.
